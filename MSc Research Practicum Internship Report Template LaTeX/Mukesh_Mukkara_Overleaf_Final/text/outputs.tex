\textbf{Generated Outputs}

The complete forecasting pipeline produces \textbf{27 output files},
including \textbf{five CSV files} containing prediction results and
response evaluations, \textbf{five JSON files} storing machine-readable
experimental outputs, and \textbf{seventeen figures} used for analysis
and visualization.

The Flask-based web assistant allows users to submit natural language
queries related to stock forecasting. The application automatically
identifies the requested stock and prediction date, aligns the date with
the closest available trading day in the dataset, executes all four
forecasting models, and presents their predictions in a structured
manner. The responses are displayed from the most advanced hybrid model
to the individual forecasting models, allowing users to clearly
understand the contribution of each component within the proposed hybrid
forecasting framework.
